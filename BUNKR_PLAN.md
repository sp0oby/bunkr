# BUNKR ($BUNKR) — Game Plan v0.1

> *"The world is ending. The chart is green. Get in the bunker, anon."*

A browser game where you run an underground bunker full of crypto degenerates through nuclear armageddon. **$BUNKR (launched on Urufu Labs, Robinhood Chain) is the game's economy:** you need it to own a bunker, it fuels your runs, it forges your gear, it's what you win, and the live $BUNKR chart is the weather in the wasteland.

---

## 1. Design pillars

1. **The token is the economy, and the game is still fun on its own merits.** $BUNKR is in every loop (section 4). The gameplay still has to be good enough that people would play it anyway, because that's what keeps the token alive after launch week. A short free demo ("Tent Mode") brings in new players who then buy in.
2. **Unhinged, but written.** Every joke is written on purpose. No "Lorem degen ipsum". If a line isn't funny, it gets cut.
3. **Short runs, lots of replays.** A 20–30 minute roguelite run. Every run is different, every loss teaches you something, and every win unlocks something weird.
4. **One look, held everywhere.** One art bible, one palette, one voice. That consistency is what keeps it from looking like AI slop (section 9).
5. **Satire punches at doomscrolling and degen culture,** not at real victims, real countries or real leaders. Every nation, leader and agency is fictional.

## 2. The core fantasy and structure

You're the bunker manager. The news says nukes are coming. Your group chat says "buy the dip." Both are right.

### A run (about 25 min)

| Phase | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. T-minus (Prep)** | 3 "days", about 4 min | Above ground, in a panic. Spend your starting stack on supplies, recruit degens from the group chat, and dig rooms. The news ticker escalates every in-game hour. Prices pump as panic rises, so buying early is cheap and buying late is expensive. |
| **2. The Drop** | 20 sec cinematic | The nuke hits. White flash, camera shake, a Higgsfield cinematic, HATCH says "gm." Anyone still on the surface is "liquidated." |
| **3. Underground** | Days 1–30, about 40 sec each | Real-time room management, 1–3 **event cards** a day, expeditions, and a **boss day** every 10 days. |
| **4. Surfacing** | 1 min | Climb out. Final score = **Net Worth After Apocalypse (NWAA)**. You get an ending card, unlocks, and a shareable "PnL card" screenshot. |

### Why it replays well

- **DEFCON difficulty ladder (5 → 1).** Beating DEFCON 5 unlocks DEFCON 4, and each level stacks a modifier ("raiders bring friends", "Ramen spoils", "HATCH is now passive-aggressive").
- **Synergy builds.** Dweller traits × room mods × **Bags** (relics) combine into builds that break the game (section 4).
- **Procedural events.** 150+ hand-written cards, weighted by state. You won't see the same run twice.
- **Daily Doom.** One shared seed per day, one attempt, and a global leaderboard.
- **Weekly mutators.** "Bear Market" (all prices ×2), "Bull Run" (everything goes up, *including radiation*), "Airdrop Season" (random loot from the sky, some of it live ordnance).
- **Multiple endings,** some secret: *Touched Grass*, *Became the Raiders*, *Rugged Your Own Bunker*, *Exit Liquidity* (you sold the bunker to the raiders), and the secret **Moon Mission**: build the Rocket Room across several runs and leave Earth.
- **A meta unlock tree.** New dweller archetypes, bunker blueprints, starting Bags, HATCH voice packs.

## 3. Systems

### 3.1 Resources (each one is a ticker with its own candlestick chart in the HUD)

| Resource | What it is | Joke |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramen** | Food, *and also the in-run currency* | "The Ramen Standard." Every purchase means eating less, which is the core tension. |
| **Rad-Water** | Water | Filtered in the "Liquidity Pool" room. Unfiltered water gives a mutation roll. |
| **Hopium** | Power | Generated by the Hopium Reactor. Low power means lights flicker and doors jam. |
| **Copium** | Stabilizes morale | Brewed in the Copium Den. Overdosing gives the "This Is Fine" status. |
| **Rads** | Global danger level | Shown as a chart that only goes up. "Number go up." |

### 3.2 The vibe meter: **IT'S SO OVER ⟷ WE'RE SO BACK**

Morale is a pendulum that keeps swinging. Both extremes are bad:
- **Too Over:** dwellers panic-sell (leave), stop working, write farewell threads.
- **Too Back:** euphoria. Dwellers YOLO onto the surface without gear, leverage the Ramen supply, and start "a new project."
- **The sweet spot** is a narrow band in the middle. Copium, the radio station, and the "Touch Grass Hydroponics" room push it around. Managing the pendulum is the real skill in the game.

### 3.3 Dwellers (degens)

Each one has an **archetype**, one **positive trait**, one **cursed trait**, and stats (Grind, Brain, Vibes, Hands). Hands goes from 💎 to 📄.

Starting archetypes (more unlock later):
- **Laser-Eyes Maxi.** Immune to panic, refuses to work in any room that isn't orange.
- **Paper Hands Pete.** Fastest worker in the bunker, flees the instant a raid starts.
- **Airdrop Farmer.** Finds free loot on expeditions; 10% of it is a trap.
- **Mid-Curve Max.** Exactly average at everything. Cannot die on the first 3 days (bell-curve protection).
- **Gigabrain Quant.** Big research bonus, eats 3× the Copium, sometimes "backtests" a room and turns it off.
- **Reformed Rug Puller.** Elite scavenger. Every day there's a % chance they "migrate liquidity" (steal stuff).
- **Reply Guy.** Boosts the morale of whoever is next to them by being unbearable.
- **Unpaid Intern from a DAO.** Works for free. Nobody knows who hired them.
- **Exit Liquidity Larry.** Shows up whenever you're about to win.

Death is "**getting liquidated.**" Funerals have an **[F]** button. Pressing F gives a tiny morale boost, because the bunker is a community.

### 3.4 Rooms

These are dug into a cutaway ant-farm grid. Merging 2–3 neighboring rooms of the same type gives bonuses.

| Room | Function | Unlocked mod examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hopium Reactor | Power | *Leverage Coil*: 3× output, 5% meltdown chance a day |
| Ramen Kitchen | Food and currency | *Instant Noodle Futures*: borrow tomorrow's Ramen today |
| Liquidity Pool | Water | *Impermanent Loss Filter* |
| Copium Den | Morale | *Hopium/Copium Swap* |
| Cold Storage | Protects stock from raids | *Seed Phrase Tattoo Parlor* |
| Shill Tower (radio) | Recruits and raises morale | Pick a station: Hopium FM, Bear Market Blues, Doomer Wave |
| Rug Detector | Raid defense | *Honeypot Trap*: raiders get stuck |
| Meme Lab | Research and crafting | Crafts Bags |
| Touch Grass Hydroponics | Pulls morale toward the center | — |
| Silo (late) | Weapon for bosses and PvP | — |
| Rocket Room (secret, meta) | The Moon Mission ending | — |

### 3.5 Bags (relics)

You "hold bags." Each run you can carry 5, and carrying more than 5 gives you the **Bagholder** debuff (slower dwellers). Examples:
- **Hardware Wallet Necklace.** Survive one liquidation per run.
- **Signed Whitepaper (blank).** All research costs nothing, but it might be a scam.
- **Lambo Keys (no Lambo).** Expeditions are 30% faster and 0% safer.
- **Ledger of a Dead Man.** +200 Ramen, haunted.
- **Laser Pointer.** Gives any dweller laser eyes.
- **Funds Are SAFU Lockbox.** Cold storage can't be raided. Opening it takes 3 days.
- **Expired MRE NFT.** Right-click to save it. Eating it is a mistake.
- **"Few Understand" Scroll.** +1 Brain to every dweller with under 2 Brain.

That's 60+ at launch, designed around synergies (for example a full Laser-Eyes crew plus the Laser Pointer plus Hopium FM).

### 3.7 Expeditions

Send 1–3 dwellers to places on the surface: the **Ruins of a Delisted Exchange**, an **Abandoned Mining Farm** (still warm), a **Collapsed Stablecoin Peg**, an **Influencer's Bunker Mansion** (it has a ring light), and **Ground Zero** (big loot, big rads). Expeditions play out as a quick text log with Bag drops and the occasional choice card.

### 3.8 Boss days (every 10 days)

- **Day 10: The Liquidators.** A raider clan with leverage. Survive 3 waves.
- **Day 20: The S.E.C. (Surface Enforcement Corps).** They audit your bunker. Anything "unregistered" gets confiscated unless you pass a fast-paced compliance minigame.
- **Day 30: The Mutant Whale.** One massive sell wall of a creature. Every room contributes damage.

### 3.9 HATCH, the bunker AI narrator

A sarcastic bunker operating system that comments on everything and has a recorded voice (Higgsfield). It is the game's personality. Examples: "Reactor at 3%. Have you tried believing harder?" / "Congratulations. You are now the raiders." / "Dwellers report the ramen tastes like regret. Regret is not a nutrient."

## 4. The token layer: $BUNKR

**Chain:** Robinhood Chain (an Arbitrum Orbit L2, EVM, chain ID 4663, gas paid in ETH). It works with viem, wagmi and Foundry. The token launches on Urufu Labs, and our game contracts only *read* and *use* the standard ERC-20.

### 4.1 The token loop at a glance

```
 BUY $BUNKR ──► BUNKER DEED (hold) ──► RATION TICKETS (spend per run) ──► PLAY
      ▲                                                                 │
      │                                       FORGE BAGS (burn) ◄───────┤
      │                                       NUKES / RAIDS (burn+pot) ◄┤
      └──── WIN $BUNKR (Daily pot, Season pot, bounties, PvP loot) ◄────┘
```
Every loop either **locks** tokens (Deeds, Subs, Treaties), **burns** them (Bags, nukes, cosmetics, broken gear) or **redistributes** them to the best players (pots). Supply gets squeezed from three directions.

### 4.2 Bunker Deed: holding is how you get in
- **Tent Mode (free, no wallet):** a demo. You play the 3-day Prep phase and the first 3 underground days. Then HATCH says "Tent detected. Structural integrity: vibes." and the run ends. That's the funnel.
- **Holding $BUNKR = owning a bunker.** Your wallet balance sets your **Deed tier**, which is read live from the chain with no transaction needed:

| Tier | Hold | You get |
|---|---|---|
| **Shed** | small | Full game, 1 bunker slot, standard rooms |
| **Basement** | more | 2 bunker slots, +1 Bag slot, Silo room unlocked |
| **Bunker** | more | 3 slots, Rocket Room blueprint, Wasteland entry |
| **Silo** | whale | Everything, gold nameplate, a custom HATCH line about how rich you are |

- If you sell below a tier, your bunker gets **"foreclosed"**: it's locked until you buy back in. HATCH roasts you on the way out. That makes holding the rational move.

### 4.3 Ration Tickets: $BUNKR fuels every run
- Each run (outside Tent Mode) costs **1 Ration Ticket.** You buy tickets with $BUNKR, and **a share is burned and a share feeds the pots.**
- You get **1 free ticket per day per Deed**, so holders can always play daily. Grinders and sweats buy more.
- **Daily Doom:** its own entry fee (10,000 $BUNKR to start: 80% to the day's pot, 20% burned), one attempt a day, so the pot grows with every player. Bought tickets add their pot share too, and anyone can top it up: the 1% creator fee goes here. The top 3 split it 50/30/20.

### 4.4 Forged Bags: burn to power up, lose them when you die
- Every run drops **Bag blueprints.** To make a Bag permanent, **forge it by burning $BUNKR.** Forged Bags are on-chain items (ERC-1155) you can bring into future runs and trade.
- **When you get liquidated (lose a run) while carrying a forged Bag, there's a chance it breaks** and is gone. That makes it a constant token sink and gives every run real stakes ("do I bring my Hardware Wallet Necklace into DEFCON 1?").
- Rarer Bags need higher Deed tiers to equip, which is another reason to hold.
- **Balance:** Daily Doom and ranked ladders use a "standard issue" loadout, so skill decides the leaderboard. Forged Bags are for normal runs and the Wasteland.

### 4.5 The chart is the weather
The **live $BUNKR price** drives the wasteland conditions for everyone:
- **Pumping (24h green):** "BULL RUN WEATHER". More loot, more morale, *and more radiation*. Everyone's "so back."
- **Dumping (24h red):** "NUCLEAR WINTER". Scarcer loot, morale drops, but raiders are weaker and **score multipliers go up** (buy-the-dip runs score more).
- **Big buys** fire a global "WHALE SIGHTED" event in everyone's game. **Big sells** trigger a "PAPER HANDS STORM".
- The community has an in-game reason to watch, meme and pump the chart together, and a red day is still fun to play.

### 4.6 Cosmetics (burn-to-drip)
Burn $BUNKR for bunker themes (Gold Bunker, Fast-Food Back-Room Bunker, Gamer Bunker with RGB), dweller skins, nuke skins (your mushroom cloud is now a dog), and alternate HATCH voices. These are also ERC-1155 and tradeable.

### 4.7 THE WASTELAND: the seasonal game-theory mode (2-week seasons)

Bunker-tier holders and above enter their persistent bunker into a shared world with everyone else. This is the endgame for the token.

**a) The global Doomsday Clock (game of chicken).**
- One clock for all players, starting at 11:30.
- **Escalation** actions (building silos, launching strikes, raiding) cost $BUNKR, push the clock toward midnight, and pay well in the short term.
- **Diplomacy** actions (staking into a Treaty or funding "Peace Talks") push it back.
- **If it hits midnight → THE BIG ONE.** The season ends early. Only bunkers above the shielding threshold survive, and they split the **Season Pot.**
- **If the season reaches its end without midnight → PEACE DIVIDEND.** The pot goes to diplomacy stakers.
- So there are two winning equilibria and everyone is guessing which way the crowd goes. That's the core game theory.

**b) Mutually Assured Destruction (deterrence).**
- Launching a nuke at another player's bunker costs $BUNKR. **50% is burned and 50% goes to the Season Pot.**
- Defense works by **locking $BUNKR in a "Sub"**, which is your second-strike capacity. If you're hit while your Sub is armed, retaliation fires automatically.
- That makes it rational to lock tokens for deterrence, which reduces circulating supply. Rational actors don't fire, and everyone else provides the content.

**c) Treaties (prisoner's dilemma).**
- Two players each lock collateral to sign a non-aggression pact.
- **Betrayal** takes 50% of the partner's collateral plus a surprise-attack bonus, but brands you a **TRAITOR** in public. Traitors take +50% damage from everyone for the rest of the season.

**d) Bounties.** Put $BUNKR on a bunker's head. Whoever breaks it collects.

**e) Anti-whale and anti-sybil measures.** Diminishing returns on stake size, a per-wallet action cap per day, and a minimum bunker age or play history before you can join the pot.

### 4.8 Launching on Urufu Labs (from urufulabs.xyz)

**What their docs say:**
- **Chain and contracts:** Robinhood Chain only. Coins are deployed by an `ERC20Factory`, so they're standard ERC-20, which a game can read and use normally.
- **Bonding curve and graduation:** trading starts on a bonding curve. On graduation it moves to Uniswap (v4) with **liquidity locked forever.**
- **No admin keys:** nobody can pause, mint or change the coin after launch. Every in-game sink therefore lives in **our game contracts**, not in the token.
- **Creator fees:** the creator earns **1% of pool trades after graduation.**
- **Optional modules at launch:** a **buy-and-burn** (up to 20% of each pool buy), a **staking pool**, vesting, and Permit (gasless approvals).
- **Fair-launch option:** 800M fixed supply, a 60-second batch auction, a sniper tax that is burned, and about 8 ETH to graduate.
- **No outside audit:** their docs say their contracts have had no outside audit yet.

**Recommended launch config for $BUNKR:**
- **Fair launch:** the anti-sniper batch makes for a better story ("everyone got in at the same price before the bombs dropped").
- **Permit on:** players approve the game contracts with one signature instead of paying gas twice per action.
- **A modest buy-and-burn (e.g. 5%).** Ties the token's own mechanics to "fallout."
- **Route the creator's 1% fee into the Season and Daily pots** and say so publicly. Trading volume then directly funds the prizes, and the community can verify it on-chain.
- **Keep the creator's holdings steady through graduation.** On fair launches the 1% creator fee is burned forever if the creator sells or moves more than 10% of their holdings or held under 0.1% at graduation, and the pot plan depends on that fee.
- **Alternative to consider: DN404 (coin + NFTs).** Every X $BUNKR automatically mints a **Bunker Deed NFT** with its own art. Holding enough tokens literally gives you a bunker you can see in your wallet. This is very on-theme, but DN404 NFTs reshuffle on transfer and some trading apps handle them badly. It's worth a look; standard ERC-20 is the safer default.

### 4.9 Game contracts (Solidity, Foundry, on Robinhood Chain)
- `BunkrTickets.sol`: buys Ration Tickets with $BUNKR and splits each payment between burn and pots.
- `BunkrForge.sol` (ERC-1155): forged Bags and cosmetics. Forging burns $BUNKR, and Bags break on liquidation (via a server-signed result).
- `BunkrSeason.sol`: Daily and Season pots, Doomsday Clock commitments, payouts.
- `BunkrArmory.sol`: Sub staking (locked with an unlock cooldown), nuke fees (burn + pot split), treaty escrow, bounties.
- **Burning:** the plain coin template may not expose `burn()`, so "burn" means sending tokens to `0x…dEaD`. That is still a permanent removal from supply and is visible on Blockscout.
- **Results:** the game server is the authority for game outcomes. It signs results with EIP-712, the contracts verify the signature, and all pot math is on-chain.
- **Rollout:** Robinhood Chain testnet (46630) first, then an outside audit before mainnet. Our contracts hold player funds, so the audit isn't optional.

### Launch note
Your call on the legal side. One heads-up: prize law is about "pay to enter + chance + prize," and paying in crypto doesn't change how that's judged, so the Daily and Season pots are worth a quick review before mainnet. Holding, forging and cosmetic burns are fine either way.

## 5. Look and feel: the art bible (the anti-slop plan)

### 5.0 Genre reference (Fallout Shelter screenshots)

We borrow the **genre conventions** that make bunker sims readable. We do **not** borrow its look, characters or brand. BUNKR has to be recognizable as its own game in one frame.

**Conventions we keep:**
- **The cutaway "ant farm":** a side view of the whole bunker, with thick black rock and frame lines between rooms and the surface as a strip along the top.
- **Readable rooms:** each room type has its own lit color, so you can tell what a room is from across the screen.
- **Room merging:** 1-, 2- and 3-wide rooms. Merged rooms visibly grow.
- **Elevator column:** dwellers ride it between floors.
- **Zoom into a room:** the camera pushes into a single room for events and drama moments (a GSAP camera tween in Three.js).
- **Side-scrolling surface view** for expeditions.
- **In-room incidents:** fires, raiders and monsters show up in a specific room, and dwellers there fight them.

**What makes BUNKR its own thing:**
- **Entrance:** a **porta-potty behind a burger joint** instead of a blast door. ("Sir, this is a bunker.") It's the first thing you see and it sets the tone.
- **Characters:** **degens in hoodies, sweats, VR headsets and laser eyes**, not matching uniforms. Each archetype has a distinct silhouette. Faces are big, sweaty and expressive (paper-cutout puppets, below), never a thumbs-up mascot.
- **Mascot:** a **CRT monitor with a face (HATCH)** mounted in every room. It reacts, glitches and roasts you.
- **Room set dressing:** crypto clutter, the visual joke layer. Mining rigs as space heaters, walls of monitors showing red candles, pizza-box towers, "WAGMI" sharpie graffiti, a mattress on the floor, RGB everything, a framed screenshot of someone's all-time-high portfolio.
- **Surface:** a ruined *financial district* instead of desert. Collapsed exchange towers, a burnt-out Lambo dealership, a billboard reading "HAVE FUN STAYING POOR" half melted. The live $BUNKR weather (4.5) tints the sky green or red.
- **UI:** a trading terminal, not a retro-future wrist computer.
### 5.1 Style: LOCKED = Board B, "Bold Cartoon" (`art/style-tests/B-bold-cartoon.png`)

- **Linework:** thick, clean black outlines; flat cel shading with a single shadow tone.
- **Characters:** chunky and big-headed, with **exaggerated expressive faces** (screaming, crying, cackling). Hoodies, sweats, VR headsets and laser eyes; never uniforms.
- **Rooms:** each room type is **lit in its own saturated color** so the bunker reads at a glance:

| Room | Light color |
|---|---|
| Reactor | Toxic green `#5BFF3C` |
| Kitchen | Warm amber `#FFB347` |
| Mining rigs | Electric blue `#3D7BFF` |
| Lounge / trading | Degen purple `#9B4DFF` |
| Liquidity Pool | Aqua `#2DE1E1` |
| Rug Detector | Alarm red `#FF3B3B` |
| Copium Den | Hot pink `#FF5FB0` |

- **Shared world colors:** near-black frames and rock `#121212`, radioactive green sky `#7CFF4F`, liquidation red `#E2382B` for danger and losses, money green `#2BD96B` for gains.
- **HATCH:** a boxy CRT with a cyan screen and a simple cartoon face (two eyes plus a mouth; it pulls a different expression per mood).
- **No print texture.** Board B is clean, and we keep it clean.
- **Reference rule:** every Higgsfield asset is generated with Board B attached as a style reference, plus the character sheet once it exists.
- **UI = a cursed trading terminal.** Resources are tickers with candlestick charts, the morale meter is a gauge, popups look like exchange order confirmations ("CONFIRM: FEED KEVIN? Slippage: 2 ramen").
- **Typography:** a condensed civil-defense poster face for headers and a monospace terminal face for numbers (Google Fonts, chosen in the style test).
- **Characters are cutout puppets.** Heads are Higgsfield portraits with the background removed, sitting on code-built hoodie bodies that GSAP animates (bobbing, wobbling, flailing). This hides AI frame-to-frame inconsistency and looks intentional, like *South Park*-style cutouts.

## 9. "Not AI slop": quality rules

1. **One style reference** feeds every generated asset. Off-style output is rejected, not shipped.
2. **Every asset gets a human review.** Six fingers, melted text or wrong palette means it's regenerated.
3. **No AI-generated text inside images.** All text is real fonts in the UI layer.
4. **Every joke is written and edited by hand** and then cut hard. Cards with no punchline get deleted.
5. **Code does the motion; AI does the paint.** The animation, timing and juice are hand-tuned in GSAP, so the game *feels* authored.
6. **A tight palette and a limited asset count.** Fewer assets with a strong identity beat a huge pile of mush.
